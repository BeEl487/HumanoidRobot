Inverse Kinematics

The same closed-loop mechanism as Forward Kinematics, driven the other way around: bar C and bar D's angles are the direct inputs here, and the two crank angles (plus the 120mm bars' universal joint) are solved each frame to satisfy the same fixed-length constraints, then reported as output.

Bar C and bar D's angles (each clamped to ±35° of upright) are the direct inputs here. The 120mm bars' universal joint and the two crank angles (the 30mm bars) are solved each frame to best satisfy bar D's tip staying exactly 42.5mm from the cross bar's midpoint (not touching it) and bar C staying perpendicular to it — watch constraint error for how far off that is once a given bar C/D pose can't reach an exact fit. The cross bar's own length isn't forced to stay at 91mm either; its actual length is reported, not enforced.
